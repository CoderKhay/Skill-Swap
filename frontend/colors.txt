The landing page uses a dark violet palette:

#0B0912 — Background: page canvas, profile-card inner rows, match-score center.
#15111F — Card: cards, navbar sign-in button, hero badge, secondary buttons.
#F4F1FA — Foreground: primary text, headings, names, body copy.
#7C3AED — Primary violet: SkillSwap accent, logo mark, highlighted headline, CTAs, icons, match indicator, arrows, focus/hover states.
#FFFFFF — Primary foreground: text inside the main violet CTA and logo mark.
#A9A3BD — Muted foreground: supporting copy, labels, availability text, step numbers, descriptions.
#E5E0F0 — Accent: match percentage, offer values, avatar initials, ring/focus accents.
rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.14) — Border: card outlines, navbar divider, badges, button outlines, section separators.
#5B21B6 with transparency — Atmospheric purple: hero glow and soft card shadows.
Opacity variants such as bg-primary/10, bg-primary/15, bg-primary/90, border-primary/50, and bg-background/90 are derived from these same tokens rather than separate colors.
